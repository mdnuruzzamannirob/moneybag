By creating an account or using MoneyBag, you agree to be bound by these Terms. Please read them carefully.

1. Acceptance of terms

By accessing or using MoneyBag ("the Service"), you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. If you disagree with any part of these terms, you may not use the Service.

2. Service description

MoneyBag is a subscription-based personal and family finance management platform. It allows users to track income, expenses, budgets, and savings across multiple wallets, and to share financial data with up to 5 family members on Pro plans.

  • Free plan: 1 wallet, 50 transactions/month, 2 budgets, 1 savings goal, basic reports.
  • Pro plans: Unlimited wallets/transactions/budgets/goals, CSV import, family groups, priority support.
  • Unlimited Lifetime: All Pro features forever, including future Pro upgrades.

3. Accounts

You must provide accurate, current, and complete information. You are responsible for safeguarding your password and for all activity under your account. You must be at least 16 years old to use the Service.

4. Subscriptions & billing

Subscriptions are billed monthly or yearly through Stripe. The 14-day free trial requires no payment information. Lifetime purchases are one-time, non-recurring. If you cancel within 14 days of a paid subscription, you receive a full refund. Lifetime purchases are refundable within 30 days.

5. Acceptable use

You agree not to: use the Service for illegal purposes; upload malicious code; attempt to access other users' accounts; abuse family sharing; reverse-engineer the platform; or harass others.

6. Intellectual property

The Service and its content are owned by MoneyBag. You retain ownership of data you upload. You grant us a limited license to use your data solely to provide the Service.

7. Termination

We may suspend or terminate your account for breach of these Terms. You may close your account at any time from Settings → Data. Termination forfeits any unused prepaid subscriptions except as required by law.

8. Contact

Questions? Email legal@moneybag.app.

Your privacy matters. We comply with GDPR and applicable data protection laws.

1. Data we collect

Account data: name, email, password (bcrypt-hashed), avatar, currency, theme. Financial data: wallets, categories, transactions, budgets, goals, family groups. Usage data: IP, browser, device, pages visited. Payment data: handled by Stripe — we never see your full card number.

2. How we use it

  • To provide and operate the Service.
  • To authenticate and protect your account.
  • To send transactional emails (password reset, budget alerts, receipts).
  • To send marketing emails — only if you opt in.
  • To improve the Service through aggregated, anonymized analytics.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies

We use HttpOnly session cookies for authentication and CSRF protection. Functional cookies remember your theme and sidebar preferences. We do not use advertising cookies.

4. Third-party services

  • Stripe — payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant).
  • PostgreSQL hosting — database storage.
  • Redis hosting — caching & rate limiting.
  • SMTP provider — transactional email.
  • Google — OAuth 2.0 sign-in.
  • AWS S3 / Cloudinary — optional receipt image storage.

5. Data retention

We retain data while your account is active. Deleted accounts are permanently removed within 30 days (invoice copies retained for 7 years for legal/tax reasons).

6. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to: Access, Rectification, Erasure, Restriction, Portability (JSON export), Object, and Withdraw consent. Contact privacy@moneybag.app.

7. Security

HTTPS everywhere; HSTS enforced. Bcrypt password hashing (12 rounds). JWT sessions with rotating refresh tokens. CSRF protection. Rate limiting. Regular backups.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@moneybag.app. We also have a Data Protection Officer at the same address.

ABOUT

We believe money
shouldn't be complicated.

MoneyBag was born from one simple frustration: personal finance tools were either too complex, too ugly, or too expensive.

OUR STORY

Started in 2023, one spreadsheet at a time.

Our founder, Mira Shah, spent years managing freelance income across multiple bank accounts, credit cards, and spreadsheets. Every tool she tried was either built for corporations or so basic it was useless.

So she built MoneyBag — first for herself, then for her family, then for the 10,000+ users who trusted us in the first year.

Today, MoneyBag is a small but passionate team spread across Berlin, Austin, and Dhaka — all working toward the same goal: making money management feel as easy as it should be.

SECURITY

Your data. Fortress-grade.

We treat your financial data like a bank does. Here's exactly how we keep it safe.

AES-256
Encryption
GDPR
Compliant
SOC 2
Type II ready
99.98%
Uptime

End-to-end encryption

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Even we can't read your transactions without your password.

Bcrypt password hashing

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (12 rounds) — even if our database is breached, your password remains safe.

JWT with rotating tokens

Short-lived access tokens + automatically rotating refresh tokens. Stolen sessions expire within 15 minutes.

CSRF + rate limiting

All state-changing requests are CSRF-protected. Aggressive rate limiting blocks brute-force and abuse attempts.

Daily encrypted backups

Backups run every 6 hours, encrypted, and stored in geographically separate data centers with 30-day retention.

Responsible disclosure

Found a vulnerability? We pay bounties up to $ 5,000. Email security@moneybag.app.
