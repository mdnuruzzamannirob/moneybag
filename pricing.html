Pricing questions

Can I switch plans anytime?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from the billing page. Upgrades are prorated, downgrades take effect at period end.
What happens to my data if I downgrade?
Your existing data is preserved. New creations are blocked until your usage fits the new plan limits. Family groups become inactive until you upgrade or delete them.
Do you offer refunds?
Yes. If you cancel within 14 days of a paid subscription, you get a full refund. Lifetime purchases are refundable within 30 days.
Is there a student or family discount?
Not yet, but we're working on student discounts. Family groups are already a great deal — one Pro plan covers up to 5 members.
