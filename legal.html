2. Service description

MoneyBag is a subscription-based personal and family finance management platform. It allows users to track income, expenses, budgets, and savings across multiple wallets, and to share financial data with up to 5 family members on Pro plans.

Free vs paid features

  • Free plan: 1 wallet, 50 transactions/month, 2 budgets, 1 savings goal, basic reports.
  • Pro plans: Unlimited wallets/transactions/budgets/goals, CSV import, family groups, priority support.
  • Unlimited Lifetime: All Pro features forever, including future Pro upgrades.

4. Subscriptions & billing

Plans and pricing

Subscription plans and pricing are listed on our Pricing page. Prices may change with 30 days' notice.

Billing

  • Subscriptions are billed in advance (monthly or yearly) through Stripe.
  • The 14-day free trial requires no payment information and converts to a paid subscription only if you choose one.
  • Lifetime purchases are one-time, non-recurring.

Refunds

If you cancel within 14 days of a paid subscription, you receive a full refund. Lifetime purchases are refundable within 30 days of purchase.

Downgrades

When you downgrade, your existing data is preserved. New creations are blocked until your usage fits the new plan limits. Family groups become inactive until you upgrade or delete them.

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it. We comply with GDPR and applicable data protection laws.

1. Data we collect

Account data

Name, email address, password (hashed with bcrypt, 12 rounds), avatar URL, preferred currency, and theme preference.

Financial data

Wallets, categories, transactions, budgets, savings goals, and family groups — the data you actively create in the Service.

Usage data

IP address, browser type, device, pages visited, and timestamps. We use this to maintain security and improve the Service.

Payment data

Payment information is collected and processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

2. How we use it

  • To provide and operate the Service.
  • To authenticate you and protect your account.
  • To send transactional emails (password reset, budget alerts, receipts).
  • To send marketing emails — only if you opt in.
  • To improve the Service through aggregated, anonymized analytics.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies

We use cookies for authentication (HttpOnly session cookies) and CSRF protection. We also use a small number of functional cookies to remember your theme and sidebar preferences. We do not use advertising cookies.

4. Third-party services

We share data with these service providers, under strict data-processing agreements:

  • Stripe — payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant).
  • PostgreSQL hosting (Supabase / AWS RDS) — database storage.
  • Redis hosting (Upstash / AWS ElastiCache) — caching & rate limiting.
  • SMTP provider (configurable) — transactional email delivery.
  • Google — OAuth 2.0 sign-in (only if you choose to use it).
  • Cloudinary or AWS S3 — optional receipt image storage.

5. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days (with the exception of records we must keep for legal/tax reasons, e.g., invoice copies for 7 years).

6. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your data (available in-app: Settings → Data → Export).
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — delete your account and all data.
  • Restriction — limit how we process your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format (JSON export).
  • Object — object to certain processing (e.g., marketing).
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@moneybag.app.

7. Security

We protect your data with industry-standard measures:

  • HTTPS everywhere; HSTS enforced.
  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt (12 rounds).
  • JWT-based sessions with short access tokens and rotating refresh tokens.
  • CSRF protection on all state-changing requests.
  • Rate limiting (200 req/15 min general, 20 req/15 min auth).
  • Helmet security headers.
  • Regular backups and disaster recovery procedures.

8. International transfers

Some of our processors (e.g., Stripe, Google) may transfer data outside the EU/EEA. When this happens, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or the processor's equivalent safeguards.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We will notify you by email at least 30 days before material changes take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email privacy@moneybag.app. You can also reach our Data Protection Officer at the same address.
