Frequently asked

Do I need a credit card to start the trial?

No. Your 14-day Pro Monthly trial starts automatically on registration. We'll only ask for a card if you decide to continue.

Can I downgrade later?

Yes. Your data is preserved. New creations are blocked until your usage fits the new plan. Active family groups become inactive until you delete or upgrade.

How does the Lifetime plan work?

Pay $99.99 once. You get every current Pro feature and any future Pro upgrade — forever, for the account you bought it on.

Do you offer coupons?

Yes. Coupons can be percentage-based or a flat amount. Apply one at checkout — it shows up on your next invoice automatically.

Is my payment information safe?

All payments go through Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Stripe is PCI-DSS compliant.

This Privacy Policy describes how MoneyBag ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our Service. We believe in collecting the minimum data necessary and being transparent about what we do with it.

1. Information we collect

Account data

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and (if you choose) an avatar URL. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and avatar from Google with your consent.

Financial data you enter

Wallets, categories, transactions, budgets, savings goals, family groups, members, and settlements. This data is stored encrypted at rest in our database.

Payment data

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number, CVV, or expiration date. We only store a Stripe customer reference and a redacted card brand / last4 for display.

Technical data

IP address, user agent, timestamps, and coarse usage events (page views, errors). Used for security, abuse prevention, and product analytics.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To authenticate you and protect your account.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud.
  • To send transactional emails (password reset, receipts, alerts).
  • To send optional marketing emails — only if you've opted in.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with vetted sub-processors who help us run the Service:

  • Stripe — payment processing and fraud prevention.
  • Email provider — to send transactional and (with consent) marketing emails.
  • Hosting & database providers — secure cloud infrastructure.
  • Analytics — anonymized, aggregate product analytics.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of MoneyBag, our users, or others.

4. Cookies & tokens

We use a small number of cookies and similar storage. Some are strictly necessary (authentication, CSRF protection, session continuity); others help us understand how the Service is used. You can control optional cookies from your browser settings.

5. Storage & security

Data is stored in PostgreSQL on managed cloud infrastructure. We use TLS for data in transit, bcrypt (12 rounds) for password hashing, JWT for session tokens with refresh-token rotation, and CSRF double-submit cookies on all state-changing requests. Access to production systems is limited to authorized personnel and protected by multi-factor authentication.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we continuously work to safeguard your data and respond to incidents promptly.

6. Your rights

  • Access: export all your data as JSON from Settings → Data.
  • Correction: update name, avatar, currency, and theme at any time.
  • Deletion: permanently delete your account and all associated data.
  • Marketing opt-out: unsubscribe from any non-essential email.

MoneyBag is operated globally. By using the Service, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent safeguards to protect your data in transit.

8. Children

MoneyBag is not directed to children under 13 (or older where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us so we can delete it.
